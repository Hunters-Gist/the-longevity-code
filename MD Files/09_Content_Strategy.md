# The Sila Code Content Strategy

## 1. Content Strategy Objective

The Sila Code content strategy exists to turn the brand from an idea into a trusted authority.

The content must educate, attract, qualify, and convert people who want to upgrade their body, mind, appearance, recovery, performance, discipline, energy, and lifestyle.

The content should not feel like generic wellness posting.

It should feel like a premium self-optimisation publication, founder-led brand, and future retreat ecosystem.

The content strategy must support:

- Brand awareness
- Trust
- Lead capture
- Search traffic
- Social growth
- Email list growth
- Retreat demand
- Product demand
- Community demand
- Founder authority
- Long-term brand equity

The goal is simple:

**Make The Sila Code the brand people follow when they are ready to stop drifting and build a better personal operating system.**

## 2. Core Content Positioning

The Sila Code content should own this territory:

> Premium self-optimisation education for people who want to build a stronger body, sharper mind, better recovery, greater confidence, and a more deliberate lifestyle.

The content should sit between:

- Longevity education
- Recovery and nervous system regulation
- Performance and discipline
- Aesthetics and confidence
- Lifestyle design
- Retreat culture
- Founder-led self-rebuild
- Premium wellness product philosophy

## 3. What The Content Should Do

Every piece of content should do at least one of the following:

1. Educate the audience
2. Build trust
3. Clarify the method
4. Strengthen brand identity
5. Create desire for retreats
6. Create desire for products
7. Drive waitlist signups
8. Start a conversation
9. Build founder authority
10. Move people into the next step

If a piece of content does none of these, do not post it.

## 4. Content Strategy Rule

The Sila Code should not chase random viral content.

The brand should build a repeatable content system around the six pillars:

- Longevity
- Performance
- Recovery
- Aesthetics
- Mind
- Lifestyle

This makes the brand easier to understand and easier to scale.

The Sila Code tone should be:

- Intelligent
- Responsible

## 31. Content Compliance Rules

* Mention peptides casually
* Promote prescription products
* Promise health outcomes
* Promise body transformation

## 32. Content Review Checklist

Before publishing, ask:

1. Does this support the brand?
2. Does it connect to one of the six pillars?
3. Is it clear?
4. Is it useful?
5. Is it premium?
6. Is it legally safe?
7. Does it avoid reckless health claims?
8. Does it move people toward a next step?
9. Does it sound like The Sila Code?
10. Would this look strong on the website later?

If the answer is no, revise it.
